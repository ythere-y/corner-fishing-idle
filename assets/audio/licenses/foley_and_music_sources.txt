FOLEY
-----

sfx_cat_steal  (meow layer)
    "Meow of a Siamese cat" by freemaster2
    https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Meow_of_a_Siamese_cat_-_freemaster2.wav
    License: CC0
    Note: the source clips on ~0.05% of its samples (peak reaches exactly 1.000).
    Harmless once normalised to peak 0.26, but do not re-normalise it upward.

catch_epic (thrash layer), sfx_fish_struggle
    "Bathtub water splashes"
    https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Bathtub_water_splashes.ogg
    License: Public domain
    48 s recording, mostly still water; the build scripts take only the loudest window.

sfx_reel_tension  (creak layer)
    "Assorted creaking noises of office chair"
    https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Assorted_creaking_noises_of_office_chair.ogg
    License: Public domain
    A substitute, and flagged as one: no CC0/PD recording of actual fishing-line tension
    exists on OpenGameArt or Wikimedia Commons. Chosen for timbre (centroid 1787 Hz,
    irregular micro-motion), lowpassed to 2600 Hz and mixed 13 dB down.
    Source peak is 1.011 (inter-sample overshoot from the Vorbis decode) — normalise
    before use.


SYNTHESIZED (no external source, public domain by construction)
--------------------------------------------------------------

sfx_new_species, sfx_record, sfx_achievement, sfx_focus_reward, sfx_event_appear,

A NAMING TRAP, RECORDED SO NOBODY RE-DERIVES IT WRONG
-----------------------------------------------------

The source directory `opengameart_kenney_interface_sounds_cc0` is named misleadingly.
Its contents come from **kenney.nl**, whose bundled License.txt states CC0 (see
kenney_interface_sounds_cc0_license.txt in this folder). They are NOT from OpenGameArt's
"Interface Sounds Starter Pack" (https://opengameart.org/content/interface-sounds-starter-pack),
which is **CC-BY-SA 3.0 / GPL 2 / GPL 3 — not CC0** — and whose files are not named
click_001.ogg / confirmation_001.ogg anyway. If you ever rebuild the pack, get it from
kenney.nl. The directory name is kept as-is only because the build scripts reference it.
