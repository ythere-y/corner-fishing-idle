# 游戏封面生成说明

## 成品

- 文件：[游戏封面.png](游戏封面.png)
- 尺寸：1600 × 900 像素（16:9）
- 用途：比赛报名表游戏封面
- 性质：AI 生成的宣传主视觉，不是游戏实机截图

## 视觉选择

封面把项目最重要的四个识别点放在同一幅水彩画中：桌面角落般安静的一方水面、带背包的垂钓者、陪伴在旁的小猫，以及从河湾延伸到雪山、海岸、珊瑚与极地的环球旅程。人物位于右下角，左上留给标题，缩小后仍能快速辨认“旅行、钓鱼、陪伴”三个主题。

画面参考了仓库中的以下项目资产，仅用于锁定现有气质、人物和宠物特征：

- `assets/art/background/spot_river_bend_dusk.png`
- `assets/art/background/spot_coral_reef_day.png`
- `assets/art/character/fisher_idle.png`
- `assets/art/pets/cat_idle.png`

## 最终生成提示词

```text
Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: 16:9 landscape competition game cover illustration for the Godot desktop game "背包钓鱼手记 / Backpack & Bait"
Scene/backdrop: a quiet watercolor river at golden-hour dusk, appearing like a living page from a travel fishing journal. The foreground represents a calm desktop-corner pond. The winding water naturally leads toward distant layered travel memories: snowy mountains, an ocean pier, warm coral water, a polar lake, and a faint limestone cavern, blended subtly into one coherent horizon rather than divided panels.
Subject: one young backpacker seated fishing at the lower-right shore, with a large travel backpack and rod; a small cat sits close beside the fisher watching the bobber. A few delicate fish silhouettes and ripples beneath the water suggest collection depth without becoming an aquarium poster.
Style/medium: polished hand-painted watercolor and gouache on warm textured paper, gentle ink details, matching the references' quiet Asian indie-game illustration feel; painterly rather than photorealistic.
Composition/framing: wide cinematic 16:9; subjects occupy the lower-right third; winding river creates depth; generous calm negative space across the upper-left and center-left reserved for title typography; strong readable silhouette at thumbnail size; no mock UI frame.
Lighting/mood: warm peach-gold sunset meeting cool blue water; contemplative, cozy, quietly adventurous; low visual noise.
Color palette: warm parchment, muted teal, dusk blue, pine green, restrained amber highlights.
Constraints: clearly communicate desktop companionship, idle fishing, travel journal, world journey, collection, and gentle discovery; promotional key art, not a screenshot.
Avoid: unrelated text, logos, interface widgets, numbers, watermark, split-screen collage, hyperrealism, loud fantasy magic, combat, extra people, oversized fish, fishing boat, crowded objects.
```

标题在第二次受控图像编辑中加入，限定只添加以下文字并保持底图不变：

```text
背包钓鱼手记
BACKPACK & BAIT
```

## 工具与披露

封面底图和标题编辑均使用 Codex 内置 ImageGen 完成。最终成品保留了项目现有水彩场景、渔夫与小猫的视觉方向，但没有直接拼贴或冒充游戏运行画面。
